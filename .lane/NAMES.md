# Architectural names

Names graduate only when a measured source change moves its named architectural invariant. A parameter sweep or nonce is not eligible.

## `starkWINTER`

Status: earned in X003.

The live walk tape becomes a cold, read-only dirty-ancilla bank for endpoint constant arithmetic. Two recycled clean qubits replace each 88-clean-qubit endpoint carry lane while the dirty tape and phase are restored. The endpoint binder disappeared; replay became the new ceiling.

## `clankerFARM`

Status: earned in X004 as a retained low-Q architecture; not yet shippable.

Replay arithmetic is divided into six balanced carry plots, large product-square adds use the same machinery, and the fused correction uses three virtual-operand plots with exact incoming-carry phase repair. The composition moved the clean Teddy ancestor from Q=1321 to Q=1266 and passed the 64-lane affine/phase/ancilla gate. Its trusted fixed-nonce receipt is still dirty and its T is above target, so the name describes the architecture rather than a winner.

## `storm420`

Status: reserved.

This name is reserved for a verified composition of independent architectural cuts that clears both the lane's Q and T gates and then passes the full 9024-shot trusted gate at 0/0/0. It may not be assigned to a single cut, an affine-only result, or an intermediate dirty receipt.
