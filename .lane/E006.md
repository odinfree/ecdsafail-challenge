## Overturn and exact state

E-004 disproved the claim that a wider low reduction window preserves the
missing state. A 53-bit window left `+2^53`; widening it to 54 merely moved the
error to `+2^54`. The final modular subtraction is conditionally subtracting
`F = 2^32 + 977` from a 256-bit wrapped difference. Its low-window operation
must export the borrow crossing bit 53, not move the truncation boundary.

The smallest semantic state is one clean qubit `boundary_borrow`:

1. Prepare the 53-bit constant `F` once.
2. Perform the existing conditional low subtraction as complemented addition,
   but use an exact controlled vented Cuccaro adder with a carry-out. That
   carry-out is exactly `borrow = condition AND (low_before < F)`.
3. Controlled on `borrow`, decrement the untouched high suffix `y[53..256]`
   using complement / exact controlled increment / complement.
4. Copy the corrected low 53 bits, conditionally add `F` with carry-out into
   the same borrow qubit to erase it, conditionally subtract `F` from the copy,
   uncopy, unload `F`, and free all workspace.

The borrow qubit is the only state crossing the low/high boundary. Constant,
prefix, and copy registers are transient reversible workspace. The opt-in is
named `TLM_FINAL_Y_SUB_BOUNDARY_CARRY53=1` and applies only to
`tlm_coord_y_sub_final`; every other subtraction and all defaults remain on
the exact prior path.

4026 below the hard ceiling 921139. This is an upper bound; actual executed T
