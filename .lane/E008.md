# E-008 causal H64 characterization

Predeclared on 2026-08-23 before any E-008 evaluator or H64 measurement. This
starts from clean pushed commit
`ea93a131e2bc488bff6fa12010d1f3606c7771b0` and retains the exact E-007
opt-in without modification:

```text
SUB4_PP_REPLAY_FOLD_WINDOW=55
SUB4_PP_MUL_PLUS2F_ALIAS=1
TLM_FINAL_Y_SUB_BOUNDARY_CARRY=1
```

E-007's numeric mismatch-index subset rule is retired for this new experiment.
Changing the operation SHA rebinds every Fiat-Shamir input, so an individual
candidate shot index is not paired with the same held-stream input. E-008 uses
only complete-ensemble counts, first-failure distributions, ancilla, and
source-bound cause classification. It does not amend E-007's terminal result.

## Frozen gates

The inherited candidate passes only if:

- Q is 1272 and rounded full T is at most 920267;
- classical mismatches are at most the held count 14;
- phase-garbage batches are at most the held count 14;
- ancilla-garbage batches are zero;
- the first classical mismatch is no earlier than held shot 405.

For H64, derive one row per nonce containing classical count, phase-batch count,
ancilla-batch count, first classical mismatch (`9024` sentinel when clean), and
exact average T. The held and candidate SHA-bound ensembles are independent,
so their Poisson count difference has approximate variance `2*held`. The
three-sigma non-inferiority ceilings, rounded outward, are therefore:

```text
classical: 1054 + 3*sqrt(2*1054) -> 1192
phase:      765 + 3*sqrt(2*765)  -> 883
```

The candidate passes the aggregate non-inferiority gate only if:

- total classical mismatches are at most 1192;
- total phase-garbage batches are at most 883;
- every nonce has zero ancilla garbage;

Clean-nonce count and the sorted first-mismatch nearest-rank q10/q50 are
descriptive under non-inferiority, not pass/fail gates. Record a separate
strict-superiority label only if classical is at most 1054, phase is at most
765, clean-nonce count is at least held, and candidate q10/q50 are each no
earlier than held. These remain ensemble summaries, not paired-shot claims.

The no-new-cause gate uses a candidate-SHA-bound exact classifier and requires:

- exact complete classical fault-set equality with all 64 unchanged evaluator
  rows;
- zero unclassified faults and no cause outside
  `walk_div/replay_div/walk_mul/replay_mul/result`;
- result-channel faults at most the held count 1;
- phase and ancilla are still taken only from the unchanged evaluator.

If the classifier is not independently qualified, record cause coverage as a
blocker rather than infer it from counts. Do not adapt the repair, corpus,
quantile rule, or classifier after seeing H64.

This correction was committed before any candidate or unchanged full-evaluator
H64 outcome. One held-only call to the already-qualified classifier on nonce
`444000000000` was used solely to confirm its command interface; it introduced
no candidate evidence and the held totals were already durable. It is not an
E-008 outcome. The correction replaces an underpowered strict comparison with
the independent-ensemble three-sigma rule above while retaining the strict
superiority label separately.

## Scope

This is characterization only. Passing every gate does not authorize a nonce
hunt, predictor range run, provider/remote action, spend, benchmark submission,
or public claim. Failing a gate stops structural promotion but still records the
complete already-started H64 block. Generated ops streams, binaries, evaluator
rows, logs, and scratch classifiers remain outside Git.
